# Design

The visual language: the design system, tokens, and UI conventions. What it looks like, not how it is coded.

## System

- No mockups: design happens directly in the code. The visual reference is the Medium iOS app on Mobbin (<https://mobbin.com/apps/medium-ios-c0e5f243-4979-40c6-a7c8-c57cfef6658d/b22663f9-abed-4c19-97ce-e22e886a3ff9/screens>, account required, so human only), chosen by the user on 2026-10-05. Never a serif font, at the user's request.
- A plain white page (`Brand.background`, sidebar included): no cards; feed and suivi rows sit on hairline dividers, sections (`AppSection`) are a title over content separated by whitespace, never a card or an extra rule.
- One section-title style everywhere: `SectionLabel` (small gray semibold), for "Propos", "Verbatim", every `AppSection` and "Apparence". Pages space sections 32 pt apart (Compte, Filtrer, the save sheet); `AppSection` adds no padding of its own. List rows (`FilterChoiceRow`, `FilterSelectionRow`) align with their section title and show their state by the check, chevron or ink summary, never a gray block. "Créer un suivi" is prominent only while there is no suivi.
- Green means "you can act": `Brand.accent` (the client's green, `#17753A`, `#4ADE80` in dark) colors interactive elements only (checked chips with `Brand.accentWash` behind, checkboxes, the listening controls) plus the wordmark ("Info" in ink, "Métrie" in green). Read-only labels stay neutral: every `KindTag` is ink on `Brand.surface`, whatever the kind. The user set this rule on 2026-10-05, after per-kind colors (navy and red, then teal and amber, then green and graphite) made tags look tappable. Beyond that, color stays with broadcaster logos and destructive feedback.
- Political neutrality rules the palette: no navy ("bleu marine"), no flag-like blue-white-red pairing, no party hue (PS pink, MoDem orange, bright ecologist green, radical-left red). The user moved off blue-white-red on 2026-10-05 for that reason; the client likes green, hence one deep, slightly blue-leaning green rather than the bright ecologist green.
- The previous direction, Stoic (gray page, white rounded cards), is history with the earlier ones (editorial "Revue", Nova / Indigo) in `Docs/UX/`.
- Reading pages: bold sans-serif titles (`title3` in the feed, `title2` on the passage), airier transcript lines.
- A small in-house design system in SwiftUI, with system fonts and native controls.

## Tokens

- `Infometrie/Views/Design.swift`: `Brand` holds the light/dark color pairs, `AppLayout` the widths and corner radius. The selected segment of a capsule control (Live / Liste, S / M / L) uses `Brand.raised`, lifted from its `surface` track in both themes: `Brand.card` there turned darker than the track in dark mode and hid the selection.
- `Infometrie/Resources/Assets.xcassets`: `AccentColor`, app icon, and the `channel-<key>` broadcaster logos. Aliased keys are normalized in the model.
- Five logos are white marks on transparency (france2, france5, franceinfo, nova, m6): each has a `<key>-ink.svg` default where white becomes `#111214`, and keeps the original white file for dark appearance. Without it they vanish on the white page. A new logo must be checked on both backgrounds (`rsvg-convert` then `magick montage` on `#FFFFFF` and `#101113`).
- App icon: an "i" whose stem rises out of a sound wave, drawn by `scripts/make_app_icon.swift` in light (ink on paper), dark (paper on ink) and tinted (grayscale) variants, opaque 1024 px. `scripts/create_project.py` declares the three variants; change the drawing in the Swift script and rerun both, never edit the PNGs by hand.
- `AccentColor` mirrors `Brand.tint`. Change both together, and the asset through `scripts/create_project.py`. System alerts and menus ignore it on iOS 26 (label color only), so it is not a lever for their look.

## Components

- Shared in `Design.swift`: `ActionButtonStyle` (capsule, prominent or outlined), `AppSection`, `AppEmptyState`, `ErrorNotice`, `KindBadge`, `ChannelMark`, `PageHeading`, `AdaptiveRow` (turns into a column at accessibility sizes).
- Passage pages stay minimal: no word is highlighted before listening starts (playing, or a position past 0), since a marked first word at rest was the loudest thing on the page; the transcript box is as tall as its text up to the former fixed height, so a short verbatim leaves no gap; "Lire le résumé" and "À propos du passage" rows are 48 pt. Listening follows the text silently, and "Reprendre le suivi" appears below the text only after manual scrolling suspended it. The fullscreen text has no header line either; the listening position exists only for VoiceOver (`transcript-position`). No technical sync explanations (estimated or precise timings, retry); only feedback to a tapped word (`transcriptNotice`) is shown.
- Listening is green: `Brand.accent` (the brand green, `Brand.intervention` aliases it) with `Brand.onAccent` text colors the play / pause button and "Écouter ce passage" (`.buttonStyle(.listen)`), the progress slider, the ±10 s icons, the queue's play circle and previous / next. The feed's "Tout écouter" stays outlined ink.
- `PlaybackDock` puts two buttons beside play: ±10 s on a single passage; in "Tout écouter" (`queue: true`), previous and next lead as prominent labelled buttons around a discreet round play button, stack past the XL text size so "Précédent" never splits, and stay beside "Réessayer l’écoute" so a failing passage never blocks the queue.
- Icon-only toolbar actions (the cross closing "Tout écouter") use the regular glass circle of the system back button: a plain `Button` with an SF Symbol in `.topBarTrailing`, default shared background. Labelled actions like "Filtrer" hide the background (`.sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)`). Avoid `.confirmationAction` and `role: .close`: iOS 26 draws them as prominent white buttons.
- Motion: animate through `.motion(value:)` (`Motion.standard` 0.32 s smooth, `Motion.quick` 0.22 s snappy), which stays still with Reduce Motion. The Actifs / Archivés chips, the feed's type chips and the feed's cards never animate: the user asked for none, and the sliding underline glitched when the pinned row jumped; each `AppTabButton` draws its own static underline. The S / M / L pill slides (`matchedGeometryEffect`), other lists fade, play/pause morphs (`symbolEffect(.replace)`), times roll (`numericText`), buttons sink under the finger (`ActionButtonStyle`, `PressableStyle`). Never animate a container holding a pinned section header (the feed's type row): the header sticks, then snaps. Scope `.motion` to the rows elsewhere, and move a scroll view with pinned headers only by instant jumps (`Transaction.disablesAnimations`); the feed jumps to the top when its criteria change. Haptics follow user gestures only: `.sensoryFeedback(.selection)` on choices, a light impact on player presses.
- Passage metadata in `PassageMetadata.swift`, all in the order of the user's own models, no avatar anywhere. Feed rows (`FeedCard`), in two groups (4 pt inside, 12 pt between, 16 pt above and below the hairline: what and when, then who and what was said): `KindTag` (the kind as a neutral caption pill, no icon, no citation mention: the user struck it out) with the channel logo at 24 pt on the right; date (plus the channel name only when no logo is bundled) and duration, no medium label; person · party (`PassageSpeaker`, the party moves below the name rather than leave a lone "·"); role; title. Sequence and podcast pages (`PassageHeading`), in groups on the 8 pt grid (context: 12 inside; "Propos" and "Verbatim": label then content, 8 inside; 24 between groups), with one `SectionLabel` style for both section titles: `KindTag`, date and duration on one line with the 24 pt logo on the right; person · party and role; "Propos" above the title; then the media, and the transcript under a "Verbatim" title (`TranscriptView`, not in fullscreen). Every variant writes the date `25/09 14:39` through `PassageDate`, with no separator before the duration (`PassageDuration`). The fullscreen text shows the medium with `ChannelSource` (logo, or the name when no logo is bundled).
- No spinners: loading states use `.skeleton()` (`Skeleton` in `Design.swift`), which draws the real layout redacted into pulsing shapes, static with Reduce Motion, and sets a VoiceOver label such as "Chargement du fil". `FeedSkeleton` renders stand-in `FeedCard`s, so it follows card changes. A control in progress (the login button) only pulses: `.skeleton(active, shapes: false)`.
- X appears everywhere as `MarkTile` in `Design.swift`: the `x.logo` custom symbol on an inverted-ink tile (`Brand.primary` behind, `Brand.primaryForeground` for the mark), at full contrast whatever the selection. The feed's X checkbox is the exception: the bare mark, since an ink tile beside a filled checkbox read as a second box. The cards and the passage pages use the tile through `ChannelMark`, which draws it for any tweet or `x` media or channel key. In feed cards the X tile sits on the right like a channel logo, after the "Publication X" badge. There is no `channel-x` image asset: `channelLogoAsset` still names it, and `ChannelMark` intercepts X before looking it up.
- Chips (`.chip(selected:)` in `Design.swift`: pale green when selected, outlined otherwise) are the one look for choices that narrow a list: the feed's types and Mes suivis' Actifs / Archivés. Each suivi lists its kinds as neutral `KindTag`s, as in the feed.
- The feed filters its types with checkbox chips, not tabs (`FeedKindPicker` in `FeedView.swift`): Interventions, Citations and X, combinable, with no "Tous" (all checked). Every chip has a green checkbox and the green wash when checked, a hairline outline when not (40 pt drawn, 48 pt to touch), whatever the kind. More categories are coming, so the chips sit in one horizontal scroll that draws out to the screen edges (`scrollClipDisabled`); at accessibility sizes they stack.
- The pinned header's background spans the whole scroll container (`containerRelativeFrame`), not just the content column: otherwise cards scrolling under it showed in the side margins, which read as the header being cut before the screen edge. The last checked box is disabled, so the feed never empties by mistake. No kind icons beside the labels: with them the row overflowed the iPhone width and hid X. VoiceOver reads "Coché" / "Non coché".
- Above the cards, `FeedStatus` reads "50 résultats · à l’instant", then "il y a 3 min" or "il y a 2 h" (age of the last refresh); it replaced the "Le fil" heading with its "24 h · N passages", and the bottom "Mis à jour à". "Tout écouter" ends that line ("Filtrer" too on iPad), outlined like the secondary action buttons so it does not outweigh the count. The user wants that text on one line: the long "il y a moins d’une minute" did not fit beside the button on iPhone, hence the short wording. When text and button still cannot share the line, the button moves below; the text wraps only at accessibility sizes.
- Under the type checkboxes, `FeedViewOptions` is one row of underlined `AppTabButton` tabs on a hairline, like Actifs / Archivés: Période (Live · 7 j · 30 j) on the left, Affichage (Liste · Graphique) as two icons (`list.bullet`, `chart.bar.xaxis`) on the right. One selection mark for every single choice of the header; gray capsule tracks made that band the heaviest for choices that mostly wait. At the largest text sizes the icons move under the periods.
- Header rhythm on an 8 pt grid: 8 above the tab row, 16 from its hairline to the results line, 24 to the first row. Checkbox labels keep one regular weight; the colored box carries the state. Only the type checkboxes stay pinned (`feed-filter-bar`); the period bar scrolls with the list, so the cards keep the room. Only Live and Liste work; the others stay visible in secondary ink, read "Bientôt disponible" to VoiceOver, and open a "Bientôt disponible" alert when tapped. No explanatory sentence on screen: the user found it took too much room.
- Main navigation icons: Le fil `house`, Mes suivis `bookmark`, Compte `person`: plain silhouettes of even weight (the user found `newspaper` and `binoculars` too busy). Filled when active, outlined otherwise, in the tab bar (`tabLabel` resets `symbolVariants`, since the tab bar fills every icon by default) and the iPad sidebar.
- Tab icons (`AppTabButton`, the feed's period tabs) sit one text style above their label (`subheadline` next to a `footnote` label) in semibold, whatever the selection.
- Custom symbols have a medium scale only: `.imageScale(.small)` or `.large` renders nothing. Size them with `.font(...)`.
- `x.logo.svg` is an SF Symbols template (v3) with a single Regular-M variant, generated by `scripts/make_x_logo_symbol.py`: never edit the SVG by hand, change the script's constants and rerun it. The mark is 90 units tall, the size of `square.grid.2x2` next to it, and centered on the cap height; at 78 units it looked smaller than the neighboring icons.
- The official mark is too thin at tab size, so the script thickens every stroke by `BOLDEN` (36 units of the 1200-unit logo; 24 was still too faint) while keeping its hollow stroke. `BOLDEN = 0` reproduces the official weight exactly.

## Accessibility

- Semantic Dynamic Type everywhere, never capped. The S / M / L reading size (default M) is relative to the system size: one step below, at, one step above (`ReadingSizeModifier`), so it works on devices already set to larger text. Accessibility sizes are never reduced, and a standard size never tips into them.
- Touch targets of at least 44 pt. Main buttons are 52 pt high, the ±10 s skip buttons 56 pt.
- Secondary text at a contrast of at least 4.5:1 (`Brand.secondaryOnSurface` on shaded surfaces).
- Rows switch to a vertical layout at accessibility sizes. Interactive elements carry an `accessibilityIdentifier`, which the UI tests rely on.
- Check each visual change in light, dark and the largest text size, on iPhone and iPad.
