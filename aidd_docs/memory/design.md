# Design

The visual language: the design system, tokens, and UI conventions. What it looks like, not how it is coded.

## System

- No mockups: design happens directly in the code. For an open visual choice (palette, logo), render the options first as a sheet from a throwaway SwiftUI script run with `swift` (`ImageRenderer`), in light and dark and at real size, and let the user pick before changing the app. The visual reference is the Medium iOS app on Mobbin (<https://mobbin.com/apps/medium-ios-c0e5f243-4979-40c6-a7c8-c57cfef6658d/b22663f9-abed-4c19-97ce-e22e886a3ff9/screens>, account required, so human only), chosen by the user on 2026-10-05. Never a serif font, at the user's request.
- A plain white page (`Brand.background`, sidebar included): no cards; feed and suivi rows sit on hairline dividers, sections (`AppSection`) are a title over content separated by whitespace, never a card or an extra rule.
- Wording: the UI says "recherche", never "filtre" (2026-10-07): the toolbar button is "Rechercher" with a magnifying glass (filled circle when criteria are active), the sheet "Recherche" / "Rechercher", "Modifier la recherche", "Réinitialiser la recherche". Code keeps `filters` / `SearchFilters` and the `edit-filters` identifier.
- One section-title style everywhere: `SectionLabel` (small gray semibold), for every `AppSection` and "Apparence". Pages space sections 32 pt apart (Compte, Recherche, the save sheet); `AppSection` adds no padding of its own. List rows (`FilterChoiceRow`, `FilterSelectionRow`) align with their section title and show their state by the check, chevron or ink summary, never a gray block. "Créer un suivi" is prominent only while there is no suivi.
- Color tells the passage kind, at the client's request (2026-10-07, after a sheet of ten styles judged ugly, a GitHub-Primer sheet judged too GitHub, then four blue-and-violet variants): interventions in cobalt (`Brand.intervention`, `#2959C9`, `#8DB0FF` in dark), citations in lilac (`Brand.citation`, `#6E4DBF`, `#B9A2F5` in dark), X in ink; the coming Instagram type takes raspberry (`#B3326F`, `#F28DBE` in dark). `KindTag` is a pale tile with the kind's pictogram (waveform, quote bubble, X mark) followed by the kind's name in small spaced capitals, both in the kind's color: the user combined the colors of variant 3 with the icons of variant 4. The kind also colors its checkbox and chip wash in the feed and a 4 pt bar down the left of its feed rows (`FeedItem.color(ofKind:)` / `wash(ofKind:)` / `symbol(ofKind:)`). Text reaches 5.2:1 or more on its wash. The logo, the app icon, primary calls to action and the listening controls are ink. GitHub's exact hues, solid state pills with glyphs, gray page and bordered list block read as GitHub: avoid them.
- Political neutrality rules the palette: no navy ("bleu marine"), no flag-like blue-white-red pairing, no party hue (PS pink, MoDem orange, bright ecologist green, radical-left red). Blue-white-red, then teal and amber, then a brand green were tried on 2026-10-05 and dropped. Kind hues also stay clear of Instagram's warm colors so its tab remains recognizable.
- The previous direction, Stoic (gray page, white rounded cards), is history with the earlier ones (editorial "Revue", Nova / Indigo) in `Docs/UX/`.
- Reading pages: bold sans-serif titles (`title3` in the feed, `title2` on the passage), airier transcript lines.
- A small in-house design system in SwiftUI, with system fonts and native controls.

## Tokens

- `Infometrie/Views/Design.swift`: `Brand` holds the light/dark color pairs, `AppLayout` the widths, margins and corner radius. Every iPhone page uses `AppLayout.margin` (16 pt, iOS's standard and the navigation bar items' inset) on both sides, reduced from 20 pt on 2026-10-07 to give text more room on the client's iPhone 13 mini; iPad keeps `AppLayout.wideMargin`. Feed row text starts 12 pt after the kind bar. The selected segment of a capsule control (Live / Liste, S / M / L) uses `Brand.raised`, lifted from its `surface` track in both themes: `Brand.card` there turned darker than the track in dark mode and hid the selection.
- `Infometrie/Resources/Assets.xcassets`: `AccentColor`, app icon, and the `channel-<key>` broadcaster logos. Aliased keys are normalized in the model.
- Five logos are white marks on transparency (france2, france5, franceinfo, nova, m6): each has a `<key>-ink.svg` default where white becomes `#111214`, and keeps the original white file for dark appearance. Without it they vanish on the white page. A new logo must be checked on both backgrounds (`rsvg-convert` then `magick montage` on `#FFFFFF` and `#101113`).
- Wordmark (`Wordmark` / `BrandTile` in `Design.swift`), chosen by the user on 2026-10-05 from two sheets of options: an "i" (stem and dot) on an ink speech bubble (rounded tile, sharp bottom-left corner), then lowercase "info" heavy in ink and "métrie" regular, both in ink (light read too thin; green on "métrie" was dropped, so the tile is ink too since 2026-10-07). The tile scales with the text size; VoiceOver reads "InfoMétrie". In the navigation chrome (journal, Mes suivis, Compte, iPad sidebar) the name stands alone (`showsTile: false`, the user's pick on 2026-10-07 among five headers): the ink tile was the darkest block on screen and echoed the X logo tiles. The tile stays on the login and launch screens.
- App icon: the wordmark's pictogram, a white "i" on an ink speech bubble (sharp bottom-left corner), centred on paper; dark: a paper bubble on ink; tinted: grayscale. Drawn by `scripts/make_app_icon.swift` with the proportions of `BrandTile`, opaque 1024 px. A full-color background with a white bubble was avoided: it reads like a messaging app. `scripts/create_project.py` declares the three variants; change the drawing in the Swift script and rerun both, never edit the PNGs by hand.
- `AccentColor` mirrors `Brand.tint`. Change both together, and the asset through `scripts/create_project.py`. System alerts and menus ignore it on iOS 26 (label color only), so it is not a lever for their look.

## Components

- Shared in `Design.swift`: `ActionButtonStyle` (capsule, prominent or outlined), `AppSection`, `AppEmptyState`, `ErrorNotice`, `KindBadge`, `ChannelMark`, `PageHeading`, `AdaptiveRow` (turns into a column at accessibility sizes).
- Passage pages stay minimal: no word is highlighted before listening starts (playing, or a position past 0), since a marked first word at rest was the loudest thing on the page; the transcript box is as tall as its text up to the former fixed height, so a short verbatim leaves no gap; "Lire le résumé" and "À propos du passage" rows are 48 pt. Listening follows the text silently, and "Reprendre le suivi" appears below the text only after manual scrolling suspended it. The fullscreen text has no header line either; the listening position exists only for VoiceOver (`transcript-position`). No technical sync explanations (estimated or precise timings, retry); only feedback to a tapped word (`transcriptNotice`) is shown.
- `PlaybackDock` puts two buttons beside play: ±10 s on a single passage; in "Tout écouter" (`queue: true`), previous and next lead as prominent labelled buttons around a discreet round play button, stack past the XL text size so "Précédent" never splits, and stay beside "Réessayer l’écoute" so a failing passage never blocks the queue.
- Icon-only toolbar actions (the cross closing "Tout écouter") use the regular glass circle of the system back button: a plain `Button` with an SF Symbol in `.topBarTrailing`, default shared background. Labelled actions like "Rechercher" hide the background (`.sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)`). Avoid `.confirmationAction` and `role: .close`: iOS 26 draws them as prominent white buttons.
- Motion: animate through `.motion(value:)` (`Motion.standard` 0.32 s smooth, `Motion.quick` 0.22 s snappy), which stays still with Reduce Motion. The Actifs / Archivés chips, the feed's type chips and the feed's cards never animate: the user asked for none, and the sliding underline glitched when the pinned row jumped; each `AppTabButton` draws its own static underline. The S / M / L pill slides (`matchedGeometryEffect`), other lists fade, play/pause morphs (`symbolEffect(.replace)`), times roll (`numericText`), buttons sink under the finger (`ActionButtonStyle`, `PressableStyle`). Never animate a container holding a pinned section header (the feed's type row): the header sticks, then snaps. Scope `.motion` to the rows elsewhere, and move a scroll view with pinned headers only by instant jumps (`Transaction.disablesAnimations`); the feed jumps to the top when its criteria change. Haptics follow user gestures only: `.sensoryFeedback(.selection)` on choices, a light impact on player presses.
- Passage metadata in `PassageMetadata.swift`, all in the order of the user's own models, no avatar anywhere. Feed rows (`FeedCard`), in two groups (4 pt inside, 12 pt between, 16 pt above and below the hairline: what and when, then who and what was said): `KindTag` (the kind as a neutral caption pill, no icon, no citation mention: the user struck it out) with the channel logo at 24 pt on the right; date (plus the channel name only when no logo is bundled) and duration, no medium label; person · party (`PassageSpeaker`, the party moves below the name rather than leave a lone "·"); role; title. Sequence and podcast pages (`PassageHeading`), in groups on the 8 pt grid (context: 12 inside; then the title; then the text; 24 between groups; no "Propos", "Verbatim" or "La publication" label, the user removed them): `KindTag`, date and duration on one line with the 24 pt logo on the right; person · party and role; the title with no label above it; then the media, and the transcript with only its fullscreen control above it (`TranscriptView`). Every variant writes the date `25/09 14:39` through `PassageDate`, with no separator or icon before the duration (`PassageDuration`, the headphones icon was removed at the user's request). The fullscreen text shows the medium with `ChannelSource` (logo, or the name when no logo is bundled).
- No spinners: loading states use `.skeleton()` (`Skeleton` in `Design.swift`), which draws the real layout redacted into pulsing shapes, static with Reduce Motion, and sets a VoiceOver label such as "Chargement du fil". `FeedSkeleton` renders stand-in `FeedCard`s, so it follows card changes. A control in progress (the login button) only pulses: `.skeleton(active, shapes: false)`.
- X appears everywhere as `MarkTile` in `Design.swift`: the `x.logo` custom symbol on an inverted-ink tile (`Brand.primary` behind, `Brand.primaryForeground` for the mark), at full contrast whatever the selection. The feed's X checkbox is the exception: the bare mark, since an ink tile beside a filled checkbox read as a second box. The cards and the passage pages use the tile through `ChannelMark`, which draws it for any tweet or `x` media or channel key. In feed cards the X tile sits on the right like a channel logo, after the "Publication X" badge. There is no `channel-x` image asset: `channelLogoAsset` still names it, and `ChannelMark` intercepts X before looking it up.
- Chips (`.chip(selected:wash:)` in `Design.swift`: filled when selected, with the kind's wash for a passage type and gray otherwise, outlined when not) are the one look for choices that narrow a list: the feed's types and Mes suivis' Actifs / Archivés. Each suivi lists its kinds as colored `KindTag`s, as in the feed.
- The feed filters its types with checkbox chips, not tabs (`FeedKindPicker` in `FeedView.swift`): Interventions, Citations and X, combinable, with no "Tous" (all checked). Every chip has a checkbox and a wash in its kind's color when checked, a hairline outline when not (40 pt drawn, 48 pt to touch), whatever the kind. More categories are coming, so the chips sit in one horizontal scroll that draws out to the screen edges (`scrollClipDisabled`); at accessibility sizes they stack.
- The pinned header's background spans the whole scroll container (`containerRelativeFrame`), not just the content column: otherwise cards scrolling under it showed in the side margins, which read as the header being cut before the screen edge. The last checked box stays checked when tapped, so the feed never empties by mistake; it is not disabled, since a dimmed box read as unchecked. No kind icons beside the labels: with them the row overflowed the iPhone width and hid X. VoiceOver reads "Coché" / "Non coché".
- Above the cards, `FeedStatus` reads "50 résultats · à l’instant", then "il y a 3 min" or "il y a 2 h" (age of the last refresh); it replaced the "Le fil" heading with its "24 h · N passages", and the bottom "Mis à jour à". "Tout écouter" ends that line ("Rechercher" too on iPad), outlined like the secondary action buttons so it does not outweigh the count. The user wants that text on one line: the long "il y a moins d’une minute" did not fit beside the button on iPhone, hence the short wording. When text and button still cannot share the line, the button moves below; the text wraps only at accessibility sizes.
- Under the type checkboxes, `FeedViewOptions` is one row of underlined `AppTabButton` tabs on a hairline, like Actifs / Archivés: Période (Live · 7 j · 30 j) on the left, Affichage (Liste · Graphique) as two icons (`list.bullet`, `chart.bar.xaxis`) on the right. One selection mark for every single choice of the header; gray capsule tracks made that band the heaviest for choices that mostly wait. At the largest text sizes the icons move under the periods.
- The chip row starts right under the navigation bar (no top padding, the chips' 4 pt touch margin tucked under it): about 17 pt from the logo to the chips, down from 29 pt that the user found too wide.
- Header rhythm on an 8 pt grid: 8 above the tab row, 16 from its hairline to the results line, 24 to the first row. Checkbox labels keep one regular weight; the colored box carries the state. Only the type checkboxes stay pinned (`feed-filter-bar`); the period bar scrolls with the list, so the cards keep the room. Only Live and Liste work; the others stay visible in secondary ink, read "Bientôt disponible" to VoiceOver, and open a "Bientôt disponible" alert when tapped. No explanatory sentence on screen: the user found it took too much room.
- A seen passage (opened, or played in "Tout écouter") steps back in the feed, the user's pick on 2026-10-06 from a sheet of three: name and title in `Brand.secondary` (title semibold), and "· ✓ Vu" after the date, so the state never rests on color alone; VoiceOver reads "Déjà vu" as the row's value. A long press (context menu) or a VoiceOver action marks a row seen or not seen by hand. Rejected: a check in the margin (adds a ring to every unseen row), a compacted row (hides the kind, duration and logo).
- Navigation bars are opaque in the page color (`.toolbarBackground(Brand.background)` plus `.toolbarBackgroundVisibility(.visible)`) and scroll views hide their edge effects (`.scrollEdgeEffectHidden(true)`). On iOS 27 the former `.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.hard, for: .top)` drew a hairline under the bar at rest and, in dark, a tint different from the page. The feed's pinned header background extends 8 pt above and below its frame (`.padding(.vertical, -8)`): otherwise a few points of card text showed just above its rule on iOS 26.
- Navigation-bar items (wordmark, "Rechercher", page titles) take colors fixed by their page (`Brand.fixed(_:for:)` with the page's `colorScheme`; `Wordmark(scheme:)`, `filtersButton(scheme:)`). The bar of a tab that is off screen during a theme change keeps resolving adaptive colors with the old theme: after switching theme in Compte, the feed's logo and its search button stayed invisible. Passing `.environment(\.colorScheme)` was not enough.
- Main navigation icons: Le fil `house`, Mes suivis `bookmark`, Compte `person`: plain silhouettes of even weight (the user found `newspaper` and `binoculars` too busy). Filled when active, outlined otherwise, in the tab bar (`tabLabel` resets `symbolVariants`, since the tab bar fills every icon by default) and the iPad sidebar.
- The feed's period tabs (`AppTabButton(compact:)`) read at `subheadline` (15 pt), semibold when selected and medium otherwise, with icons at `body` in semibold: the client found the former 13 pt regular hard to see (2026-10-07).
- Custom symbols have a medium scale only: `.imageScale(.small)` or `.large` renders nothing. Size them with `.font(...)`.
- `x.logo.svg` is an SF Symbols template (v3) with a single Regular-M variant, generated by `scripts/make_x_logo_symbol.py`: never edit the SVG by hand, change the script's constants and rerun it. The mark is 90 units tall, the size of `square.grid.2x2` next to it, and centered on the cap height; at 78 units it looked smaller than the neighboring icons.
- The official mark is too thin at tab size, so the script thickens every stroke by `BOLDEN` (36 units of the 1200-unit logo; 24 was still too faint) while keeping its hollow stroke. `BOLDEN = 0` reproduces the official weight exactly.

## Accessibility

- Semantic Dynamic Type everywhere, never capped. The S / M / L reading size (default M) is relative to the system size: one step below, at, one step above (`ReadingSizeModifier`), so it works on devices already set to larger text. Accessibility sizes are never reduced, and a standard size never tips into them.
- Touch targets of at least 44 pt. Main buttons are 52 pt high, the ±10 s skip buttons 56 pt.
- Secondary text at a contrast of at least 4.5:1 (`Brand.secondaryOnSurface` on shaded surfaces).
- Rows switch to a vertical layout at accessibility sizes. Interactive elements carry an `accessibilityIdentifier`, which the UI tests rely on.
- Check each visual change in light, dark and the largest text size, on iPhone and iPad.
