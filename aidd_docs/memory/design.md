# Design

The visual language: the design system, tokens, and UI conventions. What it looks like, not how it is coded.

## System

- No mockups: design happens directly in the code. For an open visual choice (logo, component, layout), render the options first as a sheet from a throwaway SwiftUI script run with `swift` (`ImageRenderer`), in light and dark and at real size, and let the user pick before changing the app. A palette is never tested on a redrawn mock-up: apply its tokens to `Design.swift` with a throwaway script, capture the real screens on the local test server with a temporary XCUITest, restore the code (`git checkout Infometrie/Views`) and assemble the captures into a sheet; on 2026-10-07 a mock-up that redrew the cards, the header and the player to show a palette was refused as a redesign. Save every sheet the user must review in `~/Documents/InfoMétrie – planches design/`, numbered in order, besides the scratchpad: the scratchpad (`/private/tmp`) is hidden from the Finder and may be emptied at restart. The visual reference is the Medium iOS app on Mobbin (<https://mobbin.com/apps/medium-ios-c0e5f243-4979-40c6-a7c8-c57cfef6658d/b22663f9-abed-4c19-97ce-e22e886a3ff9/screens>, account required, so human only), chosen by the user on 2026-10-05. Never a serif font, at the user's request.
- A plain page in blanc cassé (`Brand.background`, sidebar included): no cards; feed and suivi rows sit on hairline dividers, sections (`AppSection`) are a title over content separated by whitespace, never a card or an extra rule.
- Wording: the UI says "recherche", never "filtre" (2026-10-07): the toolbar button is "Rechercher" with a magnifying glass (filled circle when criteria are active), the sheet "Recherche" / "Rechercher", "Modifier la recherche", "Réinitialiser la recherche". Code keeps `filters` / `SearchFilters` and the `edit-filters` identifier.
- One section-title style everywhere: `SectionLabel` (small gray semibold), for every `AppSection` and "Apparence". Pages space sections 32 pt apart (Compte, Recherche, the save sheet); `AppSection` adds no padding of its own. List rows (`FilterChoiceRow`, `FilterSelectionRow`) align with their section title and show their state by the check, chevron or ink summary, never a gray block. "Créer un suivi" is prominent only while there is no suivi.
- The brand palette, the client's on 2026-10-07 (after a day of rejected sheets: cobalt and lilac, a cobalt-to-lilac gradient, five "universes", six palettes, a vermillon and periwinkle mock-up the user found redesigned rather than recoloured): vermillon `#E74732` (`#F05A49` in dark, `Brand.accent`) for actions, accent and identity; bleu canard `#356F73` (`#6FAEB2` in dark, `Brand.canard`; `Brand.canardFill` under white text) for interface marks and citations, replacing mineral taupe on 2026-10-08 at the client's request; noir chaud `#121110` for text and icons; blanc cassé `#FAF9F6` for the page (noir chaud in dark); gris clair `#F2F0ED` for secondary surfaces. Color tells the passage kind at a glance, the client's demand: interventions vermillon, citations bleu canard, X noir chaud (blanc cassé in dark). Each feed row has a 6 pt bar in its kind's color; `KindTag` is a ghost tag (its label shrinks, down to half, only when it would not fit: fixed at its ideal width, "INTERVENTION" at accessibility sizes outgrew the iPhone 13 mini and pushed the passage page, the profile and Mes suivis off the left edge until 2026-10-08): pictogram and spaced capitals in the kind's `…Label` color on a 12 % tint of the kind's color, with no outline (the user's "ghost": solid pills weighed too much in the list, an outline read as "bordered", bare text was too faint); the feed's type chips fill solid (`…Fill`) under white text. The tags' small text takes deeper steps for 4.5:1 on their tint (`interventionLabel` `#BB3520`, `citationLabel` `#2F6468`); the chip fills under white text are `#C93A26` and `#356F73`; the citation wash is bleu canard clair `#E5F0F0`; bars, buttons and the logo keep the exact brand values. `FeedItem.color(ofKind:)` / `labelColor(ofKind:)` / `fill(ofKind:)` / `wash(ofKind:)`. Vermillon and bleu canard differ in hue and in luminance, so the kinds stay apart for colour-blind readers too; the pictogram and the label remain.
- Political neutrality still rules any new hue: no navy, no flag-like blue-white-red pairing, no party hue (PS pink, MoDem orange, bright ecologist green, radical-left red). The client's vermillon sits near the radical left's red-orange: flagged to the user on 2026-10-07, kept as the client's brand. The coming Instagram type needs a hue of its own, away from vermillon and bleu canard.
- The previous direction, Stoic (gray page, white rounded cards), is history with the earlier ones (editorial "Revue", Nova / Indigo) in `Docs/UX/`.
- Reading pages: bold sans-serif titles (18 pt scaled like `title3` in the feed since 2026-10-09, `title2` on the passage), airier transcript lines.
- A small in-house design system in SwiftUI, with system fonts and native controls.

## Tokens

- `Infometrie/Views/Design.swift`: `Brand` holds the light/dark color pairs, `AppLayout` the widths, margins and corner radius. Every iPhone page uses `AppLayout.margin` (16 pt, iOS's standard and the navigation bar items' inset) on both sides, reduced from 20 pt on 2026-10-07 to give text more room on the client's iPhone 13 mini; iPad keeps `AppLayout.wideMargin`. In feed rows the kind bar sits in the page margin, 8 pt from the screen edge, and the text starts 14 pt after it (2026-10-07: hugging the text, 6 pt away, it read as part of it). The selected segment of a capsule control (Live / Liste, S / M / L, and the theme Système / Clair / Sombre, which left its menu for the same `CapsulePicker` in `AccountView.swift` on 2026-10-09) uses `Brand.raised`, lifted from its `surface` track in both themes: `Brand.card` there turned darker than the track in dark mode and hid the selection.
- `Infometrie/Resources/Assets.xcassets`: `AccentColor` and the `channel-<key>` broadcaster logos. Aliased keys are normalized in the model.
- Five logos are white marks on transparency (france2, france5, franceinfo, nova, m6): each has a `<key>-ink.svg` default where white becomes `#111214`, and keeps the original white file for dark appearance. Without it they vanish on the white page. A new logo must be checked on both backgrounds (`rsvg-convert` then `magick montage` on `#FFFFFF` and `#101113`).
- Login screen (chosen 2026-10-07): no card; a soft vermillon dawn (`LoginDawn`, stronger in dark, faded out vertically so it leaves no edge) lies behind the top of the page, under the wordmark, the `KindFan` illustration (also used by the empty journal) and the promise, then "Connectez-vous" and the form, centered vertically.
- Wordmark (`Wordmark` in `Design.swift`): lowercase "info" heavy and "métrie" regular, both in noir chaud, nothing else (2026-10-07). The user first picked a vermillon acute accent on "métrie" among four axes, then found it unconvincing and asked for one color. No pictogram anywhere in the app since then: the vermillon tile with a white "i" read as one more type checkbox above the journal's chips, outweighed the content and looked like the iOS information icon. The app icon keeps the bubble. VoiceOver reads "InfoMétrie"; the name is the same in the navigation chrome, the iPad sidebar, the login and the launch screens.
- App icon: the former wordmark pictogram, an "i" on a speech bubble (sharp bottom-left corner): a white glass bubble with a vermillon letter on a vermillon field in light, a vermillon glass bubble with a white letter on noir chaud in dark (brand palette, 2026-10-07; it was cobalt to lilac before). It is an Icon Composer document, `Infometrie/Resources/AppIcon.icon` (SVG layers plus `icon.json`), so iOS 26 and 27 draw the glass, specular light and the six appearances (light, dark, tinted and clear, each light or dark). `scripts/make_app_icon.swift` writes the document: change the script and rerun it, never edit the document in Icon Composer alone. Preview any appearance with `ictool` (inside Icon Composer.app, `Contents/Executables`), `--rendition Default|Dark|TintedLight|TintedDark|ClearLight|ClearDark`.
- `AccentColor` mirrors `Brand.tint`. Change both together, and the asset through `scripts/create_project.py`. System alerts and menus ignore it on iOS 26 (label color only), so it is not a lever for their look.

## Components

- Shared in `Design.swift`: `ActionButtonStyle` (capsule, prominent or secondary, see the action style below), `AppSection`, `AppEmptyState`, `ErrorNotice`, `KindBadge`, `ChannelMark`, `PageHeading`, `AdaptiveRow` (turns into a column at accessibility sizes).
- Passage pages stay minimal: no word is highlighted before listening starts (playing, or a position past 0), since a marked first word at rest was the loudest thing on the page; the transcript box is as tall as its text up to the former fixed height, so a short verbatim leaves no gap; the "Résumé" section is always open (no toggle, at the user's request on 2026-10-08) under the same 48 pt icon header as the "À propos du passage" row, which still folds. Listening follows the text silently, and "Reprendre le suivi" appears below the text only after manual scrolling suspended it. The fullscreen text has no header line either; the listening position exists only for VoiceOver (`transcript-position`). No technical sync explanations (estimated or precise timings, retry); only feedback to a tapped word (`transcriptNotice`) is shown.
- Actions in the brand palette: primary actions fill flat vermillon under white text (large semibold text, 3.9:1, above the 3:1 for large text); a disabled one turns plain gray. Secondary actions, the ±10 s buttons and the queue's round play sit on gris clair (`Brand.actionWash`) with noir chaud (`Brand.actionText`). The account avatar is bleu canard (`Brand.canardFill`) with white initials. Radios and checkboxes (`ChoiceMark` in `SearchView.swift`) are vermillon rings holding a dot or a check, unchecked a plain `Brand.secondary` ring (over 3:1). The active underline of tabs (`AppTabButton`) is vermillon. A glass sheen and coloured shadows on primary actions were tried and removed on 2026-10-07 at the user's request.
- Listening is a mode of the journal, not a button of one of its parts (the user, 2026-10-09, after three placements failed the same day: a link on its own row above the cards, at the end of the header's count line, an icon in the navigation bar). On iPhone it is the tab bar's accessory, like the player of Musique: expanded, a white play on a vermillon disc (`play.circle.fill`; a bare vermillon triangle was too faint on the glass), "Tout écouter" in ink, then "44 passages · 16 min" (what the list has loaded and will play, oldest first); minimized on scroll, "Tout écouter" alone beside the current tab. The words "Tout écouter" stay visible in both states: the user feared a bare play icon would not say it plays everything. A hand-drawn listening bar above the custom tab bar was tried the same day and refused (low contrast, too tall). On iPad, `ListenBar` ("Écouter ces 42 passages", duration) docks at the bottom of the journal column. Identifier `start-podcast`; it disappears when nothing is playable.
- The playback timeline is the system `Slider` tinted vermillon, glass thumb included. A hand-drawn slider exposed through `accessibilityRepresentation` broke XCUITest's `adjust(toNormalizedSliderPosition:)` ("Unable to get expected attributes for slider"), which the transcript tests rely on: keep the system control.
- `PlaybackDock` puts two buttons beside play: ±10 s on a single passage (at accessibility sizes the play button turns icon-only on that same row, keeping "Écouter" for VoiceOver: "Écouter" on its own row over the skips took half the iPhone 13 mini and hid the speaker, 2026-10-08); in "Tout écouter" (`queue: true`), previous and next lead as prominent labelled buttons around a discreet round play button, stack past the XL text size so "Précédent" never splits, and stay beside "Réessayer l’écoute" so a failing passage never blocks the queue.
- Icon-only toolbar actions (the cross closing "Tout écouter") use the regular glass circle of the system back button: a plain `Button` with an SF Symbol in `.topBarTrailing`, default shared background. Labelled actions like "Rechercher" hide the background (`.sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)`). Avoid `.confirmationAction` and `role: .close`: iOS 26 draws them as prominent white buttons.
- Motion: animate through `.motion(value:)` (`Motion.standard` 0.32 s smooth, `Motion.quick` 0.22 s snappy), which stays still with Reduce Motion. The Actifs / Archivés chips, the feed's type chips and the feed's cards never animate: the user asked for none, and the sliding underline glitched when the pinned row jumped; each `AppTabButton` draws its own static underline. The S / M / L pill slides (`matchedGeometryEffect`), other lists fade, play/pause morphs (`symbolEffect(.replace)`), times roll (`numericText`), buttons sink under the finger (`ActionButtonStyle`, `PressableStyle`). Never animate a container holding a pinned section header (the feed's type row): the header sticks, then snaps. Scope `.motion` to the rows elsewhere, and move a scroll view with pinned headers only by instant jumps (`Transaction.disablesAnimations`); the feed jumps to the top when its criteria change. Haptics follow user gestures only: `.sensoryFeedback(.selection)` on choices, a light impact on player presses.

- `x.logo.svg` is an SF Symbols template (v3) with a single Regular-M variant, generated by `scripts/make_x_logo_symbol.py`: never edit the SVG by hand, change the script's constants and rerun it. The mark is 90 units tall, the size of `square.grid.2x2` next to it, and centered on the cap height; at 78 units it looked smaller than the neighboring icons.
- The official mark is too thin at tab size, so the script thickens every stroke by `BOLDEN` (36 units of the 1200-unit logo; 24 was still too faint) while keeping its hollow stroke. `BOLDEN = 0` reproduces the official weight exactly.

## Accessibility

- Semantic Dynamic Type everywhere, never capped. The S / M / L reading size (default M) is relative to the system size: one step below, at, one step above (`ReadingSizeModifier`), so it works on devices already set to larger text. Accessibility sizes are never reduced, and a standard size never tips into them.
- Touch targets of at least 44 pt. Main buttons are 52 pt high, the ±10 s skip buttons 56 pt.
- Secondary text at a contrast of at least 4.5:1 (`Brand.secondaryOnSurface` on shaded surfaces).
- Rows switch to a vertical layout at accessibility sizes. A child fixed at its ideal width (`.fixedSize()`, or a `ViewThatFits` measured beside a `Spacer` in an `HStack`) can outgrow the screen there and shift the whole page left: give it the offered width (`.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)`) or let it shrink. Interactive elements carry an `accessibilityIdentifier`, which the UI tests rely on.
- Check each visual change in light, dark and the largest text size, on iPhone and iPad.
